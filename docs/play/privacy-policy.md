# Attic privacy policy

*Grease Pencil Studio · last updated 3 November 2026*

Attic doesn't collect anything. Here's what that means in practice.

**Nothing leaves your phone.** Attic has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere, even
by mistake. There's no account, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no tracking.

**What Attic keeps, on your phone:** the photos you take of your prints, the crops made from them, and the
details you add (dates, names, places, captions). Only Attic can read them.

**Copies you ask for:**
- *Your gallery.* By default, new scans are also saved to your phone's gallery (Pictures/Attic) with their
  details written into the file, so your gallery and backup apps can see them. You can turn this off in
  Settings. What happens to them there is up to those apps.
- *Your backup folder.* If you choose one, Attic copies your scans and details there. It goes only where
  you point it: an SD card, a USB stick, or a folder another app syncs.
- *A new phone.* Android's phone-to-phone transfer can carry Attic's library to your new phone. Attic turns
  Android's cloud backup off for its library.

**The camera** is used only while the scan screen is open, to find and capture your prints. Frames are
looked at on the phone and thrown away; only the photos you capture are kept.

**Deleting** a photo or an album in Attic deletes it from Attic and its copy in your gallery. Uninstalling
Attic deletes everything it keeps on the phone; copies in your gallery and backup folder stay yours.

**Children.** Attic isn't aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

**Changes** to this policy will be published here, with the date above.
