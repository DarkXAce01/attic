Crop marks

Before scanners, picture editors marked the frame they wanted on a print with two corners of red grease pencil. Attic does the same job: it finds the photo on the page and cuts it out. So the pencil here is the editor’s crop mark, two waxy corners, and there’s no ring.

The print has a deckle edge, the scalloped border of old family snapshots. That and the navy ground keep it apart from Sweepy’s straight print on cream. There are no letters in it, so it still works if the app gets a new name.
