Thirteen little worlds, each with its own story

Every kind is its own scene now, and the album's photo lives inside the story: the window of a cable car, the sail of a paper boat, a billboard in the desert, the screen at a campsite film night. Pick a theme above the cards and they all re-print in it. Same scenes, each theme's own hand.

On the albums screen

Three to a screen down the path, each a different shape and height, so scrolling feels like travelling past things rather than down a list. The title sits beside each, in the style the album's owner chose.

Each one moves a little: the kite tugs, the boat rocks, the balloon bobs, the lighthouse beam sweeps, the fire flickers. Slowly, and never while you're scrolling fast.
