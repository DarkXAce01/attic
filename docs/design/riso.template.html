Attic Attic printed in Riso

The first theme for the new Attic. The app becomes a little risograph print shop in the attic: two inks that overprint where they meet, tone made of dots instead of gradients, and the studio's red grease pencil marking whatever matters.

  • The inks print. Covers, islands, buttons and backgrounds are flat spot colour, a hair off register.

  • The pencil marks. Red grease pencil is the hand: it brackets prints in the camera, circles faces, ticks what's done, and draws the loop when a print lands in its album.

  • Your photos stay true. The theme dresses everything around a photo and never the photo itself.

How it moves

One big moment, a few small ones, and stillness everywhere else. With reduced motion turned on, every one of these becomes a plain cut.

Themes after this one

Sharing an album

Attic has no internet, so sharing works the way you'd hand over a real album: as a thing you give. No links, no accounts, no cloud. You send it with any app you already use.

Already there

A folder in your gallery

Every album already saves to its own folder, Pictures/Attic/your album, so Google Photos or any gallery shows it. Next: an "Open in gallery" button, the cover first, and the folder renamed when the album is.

New

Send the whole album

One album file: the photos, pages as they were, backs, stories, names and dates. Whoever gets it opens it in Attic and it's added to their albums as it was. You choose phone size (small) or full quality.

Already there

For people without Attic

The web page that opens in any browser, with stories to listen to, and the photobook PDF. Both move into the same Share sheet.

New

Pass it round the family

Send the album to a cousin, they add their own prints and stories and send it back, and Attic adds only what's new. Nothing you've written is overwritten. Same idea as the family questions page.

You send Nani's red album→Meena adds 14 photos and 2 stories→She sends it back→Attic adds the 16 new things
